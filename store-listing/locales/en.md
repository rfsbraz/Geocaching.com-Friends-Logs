## Summary

See your friends' logs at the top of every Geocaching.com cache page. No more digging through the logbook to find them.

## Description

See your friends' logs at the top of every <a href="https://prod.outgoing.prod.webservices.mozgcp.net/v1/820468b45848af2dee2a2365c2b1079cb3d633cdac6ac328304f00edce6d40aa/http%3A//Geocaching.com" rel="nofollow">Geocaching.com</a> cache page. No more digging through the logbook to find them.

Never miss a friend's geocaching adventure again!

This extension enhances <a href="https://prod.outgoing.prod.webservices.mozgcp.net/v1/820468b45848af2dee2a2365c2b1079cb3d633cdac6ac328304f00edce6d40aa/http%3A//Geocaching.com" rel="nofollow">Geocaching.com</a> by moving your friends' logs to the top of every cache listing page. Instantly see who among your friends has visited a cache and what they thought about it, without scrolling through hundreds of logs.

FEATURES
  • Friends logs appear at the top of cache pages
  • See your own logs highlighted separately
  • Configurable display limit (5, 10, 20, 50, or 100 logs)
  • Toggle friends logs and your own logs independently
  • Clean, non-intrusive design that matches <a href="https://prod.outgoing.prod.webservices.mozgcp.net/v1/820468b45848af2dee2a2365c2b1079cb3d633cdac6ac328304f00edce6d40aa/http%3A//Geocaching.com" rel="nofollow">Geocaching.com</a>

PERFECT FOR
  • Planning group caching trips with friends
  • Discovering caches your friends recommend
  • Avoiding spoilers from strangers while reading trusted reviews
  • Quickly checking if friends have already found a cache

HOW IT WORKS
  Simply install the extension, visit any cache page on <a href="https://prod.outgoing.prod.webservices.mozgcp.net/v1/820468b45848af2dee2a2365c2b1079cb3d633cdac6ac328304f00edce6d40aa/http%3A//Geocaching.com" rel="nofollow">Geocaching.com</a>, and your friends' logs will automatically appear at the top. Use the popup menu to customize your preferences.

PRIVACY
  This extension only runs on <a href="https://prod.outgoing.prod.webservices.mozgcp.net/v1/820468b45848af2dee2a2365c2b1079cb3d633cdac6ac328304f00edce6d40aa/http%3A//Geocaching.com" rel="nofollow">Geocaching.com</a> and does not collect or transmit any personal data.
